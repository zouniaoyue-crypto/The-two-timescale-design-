G20 [Moderate] I, l. 76 "the antennas have to be repositioned in each ... (CI)" -- E, REJECTED
  The positions are optimized from instantaneous CSI, and the small-scale fading is i.i.d. across
  CIs. So the optimized positions change from one CI to the next with probability one, and
  repositioning in each CI is the actual consequence. [16] states it the same way ("frequent MA
  position adjustments based on instantaneous CSI incur high operational complexity").
