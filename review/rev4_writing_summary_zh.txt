   使能技术和相关工作三层内容，读者难以抓住主线。

   小问题：II-B 协议段对 (p,q) 的前向引用不明确；(P1) 后一句与约束中的 "∀H(t)" 重复。

三、主要修改（按章节）

【摘要】（260 → 190 词）
  - 删去开头的动机句，改为导师模板：范围（where 从句说明两个时间尺度）→ 问题（目标与全部约束类别）
    → 求解主线（"To solve this nonconvex stochastic problem, we first ..., where ..."）→ 算法（含收敛保证
    与用途）→ 结果。
  - 条件独立性的证明移到引言（属于论证细节）；保留"即使单个 LU，MA 也能降低泄露并增加 AN"的洞见。

  修改前（节选）："Movable antennas (MAs) can reshape the spatial correlation ..., but repositioning them
    in every channel coherence interval incurs substantial movement and channel acquisition overhead. ..."
  修改后（全文）："This paper investigates two-timescale secure transmission for a movable antenna
    (MA)-enabled multiuser downlink with a passive eavesdropper (Eve), where the antenna positions are
    optimized based on statistical channel state information (CSI), and the zero-forcing precoding with
    null-space artificial noise (AN) is adapted to the instantaneous CSI of the legitimate users (LUs).
    Specifically, the antenna positions and the long-term power allocation of the confidential signals
    and AN are jointly optimized to maximize the ergodic secrecy sum rate subject to the transmit power,
    movable-region, and minimum-distance constraints. To solve this nonconvex stochastic problem, we first
    transform it into a deterministic problem by approximating the ergodic wiretap rate in closed form,
    where the resulting expression reveals that the MAs can reduce the information leakage and increase
    the AN received by Eve, even for a single LU. Then, an alternating optimization algorithm with
    guaranteed convergence is developed for the deterministic problem, where the power allocation among
    the LUs follows a secrecy water-filling structure, and each antenna position is updated by a grid
    search followed by majorization–minimization. Simulation results demonstrate that the proposed design
    outperforms the benchmark schemes with less AN power and fewer antennas."

  - 第 4 段（分析贡献）：问题 → 条件独立性（含"为什么需要"）→ 闭式近似 → 洞见。洞见句不再复述摘要，
